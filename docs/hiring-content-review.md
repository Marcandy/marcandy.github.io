No features, credentials, certifications or business outcomes were invented.

[GitHub's hiring guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/tutorials/using-your-github-profile-to-enhance-your-resume) supports a concise professional introduction, relevant skills and projects, clear contribution attribution, and code/demo links that are easy to explore. This pass applies those principles while following the user's explicit preference for concise, positive employer-facing copy.
